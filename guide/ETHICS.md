# Ethics, IP and security

</div>

## What the study handles

The study works on **plants, images and model outputs**, not on patients. The one human-related layer is the percipient layer: subjective impressions of volunteers on photographs of plant trays. Its files are kept in `reports/percipient_reports/`; **participant names and per-person data are not reproduced on these pages**, and the repository is private. Photographs are phone images of trays and microscope frames made by the researchers.
